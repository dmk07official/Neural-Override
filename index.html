Music

Neural Override is out here making music that sounds like someone is singing into a void and somehow people listen.

Most of it will probably make you question life choices, but hey, that's Incelcore for you.

About Neural Override

The Beginning

Neural Override started making music under this name back in February 2025.

At first, he was all into the E-Punk and Alternative scene, dropping tracks on YouTube and later on SoundCloud.

About three months later, he hit me up (Dominik) to work on a feature (Alcoholic Therapy), and from there on, I became a part of his music journey.

We were thinking about making an album and how to get our music to you. During that period of time, I created this website, and Neural Override started to connect to the Incelcore scene and worked on some features and compilations with some cool people.

In November 2025, Neural Override released his first single (I Know That I Want You) on Spotify. He started to make serious progress and improvements and is currently working on his first EP, Unloveable.

The genre of his tracks has shifted more to Incelcore on tracks he works on alone, while when we are working on a song together, it's more of a Bedroom Rock Pop vibe.

The Sound

The music is mostly rocky — somewhere between alt rock and pop punk, sometimes acoustic, sometimes a bit harder, occasionally something completely different.

Genre-wise it's Incelcore, which basically means the lyrics are about lost love and feeling kinda stuck. It won't fix your problems, but at least you won't be sad alone (which is kind of the whole point). If you want to actually hear it — all incelcore music releases are here.

Now

In Summer of 2026, Ceddo joined, and newcomer Neural Override became sort of a band.

We are looking forward to our first band practice, to make music and drink a lot of beer.

And maybe we can surpass our TikTok Livestreams (check them out, you can't sleep anyways) and perform our songs live on Stage in 2026.

So please support us by listening to our songs on Spotify (the beer prices won't go down due to war...).

For more information about us both, you can check out our members page and for the latest news about Neural Override check our news page.
