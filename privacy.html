Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, Cloudflare's servers automatically process your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and the browser and operating system you use. This is technically necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Cloudflare processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Because Cloudflare is a US company, data may also be processed in the United States; the agreement includes the EU standard contractual clauses for such transfers. More information: Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Images (ImageKit)

The photos and cover images on this website are delivered by the image service ImageKit (imagekit.io). To deliver an image, ImageKit receives your IP address and technical information about your browser. This data may also be processed on servers outside the European Union. We use the service to deliver images quickly and in a size that fits your device (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).
